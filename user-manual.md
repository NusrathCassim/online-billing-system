# Pahana Edu Online Billing System

---

## Overview
**Pahana Edu Online Billing System** is a web-based application that automates billing, inventory management, and reporting for bookstores or educational institutes. It reduces manual effort and provides real-time insights into sales and stock.

---

## Features
- **Customer Management:** Add, edit, view, delete customer records  
- **Item & Category Management:** Manage inventory, categories, pricing, and stock  
- **Billing & Invoice Generation:** Create, calculate, and print bills  
- **Reports:** Customer, item, and sales reports (exportable to PDF/Excel)  
- **User Roles:** Administrator, Staff, optional Customer access  
- **Help Section:** Guidelines, FAQs, troubleshooting tips  

---

## User Roles

| Role          | Access Level                                    |
|---------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Administrator | Full access: users, customers, items, billing, reports |
| Staff         | Manage customers, items, and generate bills   |
| Customer      | View personal account and billing history     |

---

## System Requirements
- **Web Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge (latest versions)  
- **Server:** Apache Tomcat 10+  
- **Database:** MySQL 8+  
- **Java Runtime:** JDK 17+  

---

## Getting Started
1. Open a supported web browser  
2. Navigate to: `http://localhost:8080/online-billing-system`  
3. Log in with your assigned username and password  


---

## Dashboard Modules
- **Customer Management** – Manage all customer records  
- **Item/Category Management** – Maintain inventory and categories  
- **Billing** – Create and manage bills  
- **Reports** – Generate sales, customer, and inventory reports  
- **Help** – FAQs and troubleshooting  
- **Logout** – Securely end the session  

---

## Usage Guide

### Customer Management
- **Add Customer:** `Customer → Add New` → Fill details → `Save`  
- **Edit Customer:** `Customer List → Edit → Modify → Save`  
- **View/Delete Customer:** `Customer List → View/Delete`  

### Item & Category Management
- **Add Item:** `Items → Add Item → Fill details → Save`  
- **Edit/Delete Item:** `Items List → Edit/Delete`  

### Billing
- **Create Bill:** `Billing → Create Bill → Select customer → Add items → Generate Bill`  
- **Print Invoice:** After bill generation → `Print`  

### Reports
- **Customer Report:** Complete customer database  
- **Item Report:** Inventory and stock levels  
- **Sales Report:** Billing and sales history  
- Export reports to PDF or Excel  

### Help Section
Provides usage guidelines, FAQs, and troubleshooting tips  

### Logout
Click `Logout` to end the session and return to the login page  

---

## Best Practices
- Always log out after use  
- Verify customer and item records regularly  
- Generate reports frequently
